Install OKV School

Your school has invited you to the OKV School app.

Step 1 · Install the app

  1. Android (Chrome): tap the menu ⋮ and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon in the address bar.

Step 2 · Sign in

Once it is installed, the sign-in page opens. Use the email and password you were given.

Installing does not work on my device

You can still use OKV School in your browser. Open the sign-in page and bookmark it.
